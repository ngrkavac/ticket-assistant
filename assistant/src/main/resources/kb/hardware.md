# Hardware requests and faults
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Request a replacement via the Self-Service portal, category Hardware.
If a laptop does not charge, try another charger and hold the power button for 30 seconds before
raising a ticket. Faulty devices get a loaner within 1 business day; bring the device to floor 1 IT desk.
Printer issues: check the printer is not paused in the print queue, then restart the "Print Spooler" service.
